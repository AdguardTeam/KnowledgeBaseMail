AdGuard offers two temporary email services:

- [AdGuard Temp Mail](https://adguard.com/adguard-temp-mail/overview.html) — a disposable address generator

- [AdGuard Mail](https://adguard-mail.com/welcome.html) — an email management product with Temp Mail functionality

Both services are suitable for one-time tasks like activating a free trial or getting promo codes. Here’s a comparison to help you choose the one that best fits your needs.

| **Feature** | **AdGuard Temp Mail** | **Temp mail in AdGuard Mail** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Lifetime** | up to 7 days | **Guest mode:** active for up to 7 days after your last interaction, with a maximum lifetime of 6 months **Signed-in mode:** No time limit |
| **Availability** | Only on the webpage where you open it | Web version, mobile and desktop |
| **Registration** | Not required | **Guest mode:** create and manage your temporary address, receive and read emails, and customize basic app settings without registration  **Signed-in mode:** register to save your mailbox data, sync it across devices, and unlock additional settings |

## Lifetime of addresses

In AdGuard Temp Mail, your disposable email address expires 7 days after you close the website.

Temp mail in AdGuard Mail offers two modes:

- **Guest mode:** Your temporary email address stays active for up to 7 days after your last interaction with AdGuard Mail, but a guest mailbox can exist for no longer than 6 months in total.

> Keep in mind that your guest mailbox is tied to your browser. In the web version, you can access it as long as your browser data is preserved. If you clear your cookies or switch to another browser, you may lose access to the mailbox. Once your address expires, you can simply create a new one.

- **Signed-in mode:** Your temporary email address has no time limit. You can keep it for as long as you like.

## Platform availability

In AdGuard Temp Mail, the generated email address is only available on the page where you open it. You can’t use it on another device. Once you close the page or clear your browser cache, the address and its inbox will be deleted.

With AdGuard Mail, you can use your temporary email in the web version, desktop app, or mobile app. If you sign in, you can access your inbox from any device at any time.

## Registration

AdGuard Temp Mail doesn’t require registration. Visit the website, pass the CAPTCHA, and a disposable email address will be generated.

AdGuard Mail also lets you use Temp mail without an account. Choose *Continue without account* on the login page to access the guest mode and get one free temporary email address right away.

To keep your temporary email address without time limits and unlock other AdGuard Mail features, sign in using your [AdGuard account](https://auth.adguardaccount.com/login.html).
